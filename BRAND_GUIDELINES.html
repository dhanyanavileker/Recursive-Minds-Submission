01 / The idea

Useful materials. Another beginning.

What we stand for

Re:Source helps neighbours find and share leftover materials. Lead with local usefulness, clear next steps, and practical information. Recursive Minds brings curiosity, iteration, and technical experimentation as a secondary team identity.

How we show up

Re:Source: warm, practical, community-focused. Recursive Minds: technical, bold, experimental.

Keep the identity thoughtful and confident, with generous space and deliberate use of colour.

04 / Logo system

Protect the mark.

Primary & compact

The nested head above the two-line wordmark is the primary logo. Use the head alone where the team name is already visible.

The infinity symbol is a secondary accent for sleeves, badges, and small details.

Space & reproduction

  • Leave clear space equal to at least the height of the “M” in “MIND.”
  • Prefer plain black or deep charcoal backgrounds.
  • Keep proportions, profile details, and the original colour treatment.
  • Use a solid white version when gradients cannot reproduce reliably.
  • At small sizes, remove the tagline first and check that nested lines remain distinct.

Never stretch, rotate, add extra effects, or place the logo over busy patterns. Preserve the wordmark as artwork.

Small attention and shortage cues

Neon boundary: keep the cyan-to-violet gradient only in the Recursive Minds logo/signature and small AI moments: assistant icons, active AI states, and generated recommendation accents. Product navigation, buttons, page backgrounds, and full cards stay calm.

Use charcoal body copy on cream or mint; cream text on leaf green or forest. Amber fills need dark text. Muted text: #52665D; borders: #B8CBBF. Status includes a label as well as colour.

Suggested digital values. Confirm printed colours with a physical sample before production.

Clear thinking, clearly set.

Suggested body / Inter

Every iteration takes the idea further.

Use sentence case and normal spacing for longer text. Keep information easy to read.

This file works offline and uses locally installed fonts when available, with Arial as the fallback. Font samples are illustrative; the existing logo wordmark remains artwork.

07 / Graphic language & apparel

Build with layers.

Supporting graphics

Product UI uses clear thin line icons, calm surfaces, and restrained shadows. Keep contours and circuit motifs inside the team artwork.

Keep patterns at the edges or lower part of a layout. Leave generous empty space around the logo and message. Avoid competing neon colours or dense decoration.

T-shirt placement

  • Front: Centred head and wordmark; tagline below.
  • Back: Campaign statement at the top; team name beneath.
  • Number: Treat 91 as a configurable team or player identifier, separate from the logo.
  • Sleeve: Small infinity symbol.
  • Lower back: Preserve the original team artwork; do not extend neon contours into product UI.
08 / Voice

Helpful, practical, neighbourly.

Write with clarity, curiosity, and confidence. Emphasise what the team explores, builds, and improves. Avoid inflated claims and unnecessary AI jargon.
