04 / Logo system

Protect the mark.

Primary & compact

The nested head above the two-line wordmark is the primary logo. Use the head alone where the team name is already visible.

The infinity symbol is a secondary accent for sleeves, badges, and small details.

Space & reproduction

  • Leave clear space equal to at least the height of the “M” in “MIND.”
  • Prefer plain black or deep charcoal backgrounds.
  • Keep proportions, profile details, and the original colour treatment.
  • Use a solid white version when gradients cannot reproduce reliably.
  • At small sizes, remove the tagline first and check that nested lines remain distinct.

Never stretch, rotate, add extra effects, or place the logo over busy patterns. Preserve the wordmark as artwork.

07 / Graphic language & apparel

Build with layers.

Supporting graphics

Use nested contours, flowing paths, subtle circuit details, and thin line icons to suggest depth, iteration, and connected thinking.

Keep patterns at the edges or lower part of a layout. Leave generous empty space around the logo and message. Avoid competing neon colours or dense decoration.

T-shirt placement

  • Front: Centred head and wordmark; tagline below.
  • Back: Campaign statement at the top; team name beneath.
  • Number: Treat 91 as a configurable team or player identifier, separate from the logo.
  • Sleeve: Small infinity symbol.
  • Lower back: Subtle cyan and violet contours.
08 / Voice

Curiosity with confidence.

Write with clarity, curiosity, and confidence. Emphasise what the team explores, builds, and improves. Avoid inflated claims and unnecessary AI jargon.
