# Re:Source
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users and purpose
People offering non-structural renovation leftovers and people seeking materials for small creative projects. Help them turn a project need into evidence-based inventory matches.
## Stack
User selected shadcn/ui. Prototype implementation uses React, TypeScript and Vite.
## Core journeys
Find materials first (confirmed by user), list leftovers, save a project, simulate a pickup. See RESOURCE_PLAN.md for the authoritative scope.
## Constraints
Local interactive prototype with labelled fictional inventory, illustrative locality, no live AI or hosted services. No payments, real messaging, delivery, or structural certification. Future hosting must use Google services without billing.
## Brand
Recursive Mind brand palette and typography per BRAND_GUIDELINES.md.
## Open decisions
Actual pilot locality, Firebase project, Gemini quota and deployment remain unconfirmed.
